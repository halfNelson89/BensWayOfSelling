Lesson 2 · Practice the framework

The call game

Draw a random object, read the two fact sheets, then run the whole call. At every step you pick the line that moves the customer forward. Pick wrong and you hear how a real customer would react.

How to play

  1. Draw an object. Each one comes with a customer and a situation. None of them are medical, so all of your attention goes to the method.
  2. Read both fact sheets first. The customer sheet is your pre-call plan. The product sheet tells you the one property that matters and the honest boundary. Every correct line uses something from them.
  3. Run the call. Connect, Discover, Diagnose, the "that's right" checkpoint, Align, Advance. Each wrong pick costs 15 trust. Keep picking until the call moves.
  4. Read the debrief. It replays your call step by step and lists where it stalled.

Run it in pairs during Phase 1: one rep reads the customer's reactions out loud, the other picks the line and explains why.

Primary source

After two or three draws, read chapter 5 of Never Split the Difference by Chris Voss and Tahl Raz (Harper Business, 2016) on the two words "that's right." It explains why the checkpoint round matters so much.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, ask your trainer on the next cohort call, or ask Claude to explain it another way or give you more practice lines.

Keep the field card handy for calls and ride-alongs.
