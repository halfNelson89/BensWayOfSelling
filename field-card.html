Pre-call plan

  1. Who they are and what they're measured on (AcuityMD: case mix, sites, network)
  2. Their reality, written as a hypothesis to test
  3. Parallel fact: sourced, national, parallel, line to the solution, opens Discover
  4. The opening question
  5. The Discover question that follows (write it first)
  6. Accusation audit list
  7. Objective and the Advance you'll ask for

Discover toolkit

ToolHowExample
Open questionHow, What, Tell me about. Never Why. Could they disagree without feeling corrected?"Tell me about where the time goes in a typical case."
MirrorLast one to three words, rising tone, silence"Packing and waiting?"
Label"It seems" or "It sounds like." Never "I think" or "What I'm hearing.""It sounds like that wait is where the day gets away from you."
Accusation auditSay their negative thought first"You're probably thinking I'll disappear after the in-service."
CheckpointSituation, their number, consequence, owner. "Did I get that right?""That's right" moves you to Align. "You're right" sends you back.

Listen for

Workarounds, frequency words, time and count words, emotion, other people's names, and status quo defenses.

Align formula

"Based on what you told me, [their words]. [One product] [one property that answers that pain]. [One honest boundary]."

Claims, numbers and boundaries come from the IFU and approved materials.

Advance

  • Concrete, mutual, time-bound. A dated no beats an undated maybe.
  • Buying signals sound like logistics. When you hear one, stop selling.
  • After the yes: Follow Up (do what you said, when you said), Pull Through (shelf, preference card, trained team, covered cases, review the number), Continue the sale (next case type, next surgeon, next department).

Glossary

TermMeaning in this course
Parallel factA sourced, national fact that sits beside hemostasis (cost, time, blood supply, quality metrics) and opens a conversation without asking the customer to defend their results.
Status quoThe customer's current way of working. Your real competitor. You reveal its cost; you don't attack it.
Problem framingNaming a problem for the customer before they describe it. Replaced by shaping the conversation.
Listen 70, talk 30The Discover target for share of talk time.
MirrorRepeating the customer's last one to three words with a rising tone, then staying silent.
LabelNaming what you observe, starting with "It seems" or "It sounds like."
Accusation auditSaying the negative thing the customer is already thinking before they say it.
"That's right" checkpointThe summary you give before Align. "That's right" means understood. "You're right" is a polite brush-off.
Honest boundaryThe one limit or precaution, from the IFU, that you state in Align so the claim is believable and on label.
Buying signalA logistics question or commitment that shows the decision is made.
AdvanceA specific, dated commitment that moves the sale forward.
ContinuationA conversation that goes on without any commitment. Not an Advance.
Pull throughThe work that makes a first case the default: shelf, preference card, trained team, covered cases, review.
Preference cardThe surgeon's standing list of supplies and setup for a procedure. Getting on it is the core of pull through.
